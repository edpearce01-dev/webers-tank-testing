Every Batch Documented to Federal DOT Standards

Complete traceability from morning calibration through final requalification paperwork.

Step 1 • Daily Calibration

±1.0% Calibrated Verification

Water jackets are verified daily against our calibrated master cylinder prior to testing in accordance with 49 CFR §180.215.

Step 2 • 7-Point Visual

Internal & External Inspection

Every cylinder is inspected for corrosion, dents, cuts, gouges, neck/thread defects, heat exposure, and relief valve integrity.

Step 3 • Volumetric Hydro

Water Jacket Expansion Test

Pressurized to exact DOT/CGA test specification (5/3 or Special Permit pressure) measuring total, permanent, and elastic expansion.

Step 4 • Certification

RIN B386 Stamp / Label & Log

Approved cylinders receive official B386 requalification markings or epoxy-grade composite labels plus itemized DOT test records.

Common DOT Cylinder Retest Intervals

Not sure if your department or shop cylinders are due? Call us with the shoulder stampings or label spec.

Cylinder Type / DOT Spec Standard Hydro Interval Key Compliance Notes
Carbon Fiber / Kevlar SCBA (DOT-SP) Every 5 Years Tested to REE (Rejection Elastic Expansion) limit; standard 15-year service life unless extended under applicable DOT Special Permit.
Aluminum Cylinders (DOT 3AL — SCUBA, CO2, O2) Every 5 Years 5/3 service pressure water-jacket test plus neck/thread visual check; SCUBA cylinders typically receive annual visual inspection.
High-Pressure Steel (DOT 3A / 3AA) 5 Years (or 10 Years if ★ eligible) 5/3 service pressure test; 10% maximum permanent expansion tolerance under 49 CFR §180.209.
